In one line: build a web app where employees raise purchase requests by chatting, and the

version, the app must change how it decides, without any code change.
1. The business problem
People across a factory raise purchase requisitions (PRs) every day. Procurement decides what to do
with each: buy against a rate contract, order from the preferred supplier, or get quotes first. Today
this is manual, slow and inconsistent. People split large needs into small PRs to avoid getting quotes,
and every policy change means retraining everyone.
Your app automates this for Corvo Engineering, a fictional engineering manufacturer with two plants.
Its rules are in the Procurement Policy v5.0. Read it closely: the order of the rules matters, and so do
the details.

 Policy as data, not code. The rules your engine applies (limits, windows, rule order, conditions on
